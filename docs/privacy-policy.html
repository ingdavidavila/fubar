FUBAR Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

The short version: FUBAR does not collect, send or share any personal information. Everything the app remembers stays on your own device.

Who we are

FUBAR is a pass-and-play party card game for adults. In this policy, "we" and "the app" mean FUBAR and its developer.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party advertising or analytics code. It does not connect to the internet to run.

What the app stores on your device

To make the game convenient, FUBAR saves a small amount of information in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • The player names and groups (Guys, Girls or Other) you type in on the setup screen, so you do not have to retype them.
  • Your game in progress (the deck order, whose turn it is and the last card drawn), so you can close the app and resume.
  • Your sound and vibration setting.

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it. You can delete it at any time by tapping "Discard saved game" in the app, or by deleting the app, which removes all of it.

Permissions

FUBAR does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, location or any other personal data. It uses your phone's speaker and vibration motor to play sounds and haptic feedback.

Third parties

We do not share data with anyone because we do not have any. If you downloaded the app from the Apple App Store or Google Play, those companies may collect their own information about the download and, if you have opted in, diagnostics such as crash reports, under their own privacy policies.

Children

FUBAR is a drinking game intended for adults of legal drinking age. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Play responsibly

Know your limits, drink water, skip any turn you like, and never drive after drinking. Non-alcoholic drinks work just as well.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
