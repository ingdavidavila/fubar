> No deck? No problem. FUBAR puts a full deck of cards and every rule on one phone, so the party can start anywhere.
>
> HOW IT WORKS
> Sit in a circle and pass the phone. On your turn, tap the deck to flip a card. The app shows what it means and who drinks. Keep going until the fourth King is drawn or the deck runs out.
>
> EVERY CARD DOES SOMETHING
> • Ace and 10: Social. Everybody drinks.
> • 2 and 3: You drink.
> • 4: Questions. Ask someone a question; they can only answer with another question.
> • 5: Give out five drinks.
> • 6: Never Have I Ever.
> • 7: Thumbmaster. Last to notice the thumb drinks.
> • 8: Categories.
> • 9: Rhyme.
> • Jack and Queen: The guys or the girls drink.
> • King: Waterfall.
>
> BUILT FOR THE TABLE
> • Real card flips with sound and haptic feedback
> • Random prompts for Categories, Rhyme and Never Have I Ever
> • Keeps track of the Thumbmaster for you
> • Pick a group for each player so Jack and Queen know who is up
> • Your game saves automatically. Close the app, come back, and pick up where you left off
> • Built-in rules screen, so new players can learn on the spot
> • Dark design that works in a dim room
> • No accounts, no ads, and it works offline
>
> BEST WITH 6 OR MORE PLAYERS
> One phone, passed around the table. Works with fewer, but the more the merrier.
>
> PLAY RESPONSIBLY
> FUBAR is for adults of legal drinking age. Know your limits, drink water, skip any turn you like, and never drive after drinking. Non-alcoholic drinks work great too.

## Before you submit (to-do)

1. **Check the name is free.** App names are unique per store. If "FUBAR: Drinking Card Game" is taken, try "FUBAR Card Game" or "FUBAR Party Deck".
2. **Trademark.** Confirm nobody owns FUBAR as a card-game trademark before launch.
3. **Privacy policy URL.** Both stores require one even when you collect nothing. A short page saying "FUBAR collects no data; everything stays on your device" is enough. I can write it.
4. **Support URL and contact email.** Needed on both stores. Use an address you're happy to make public.
5. **In-app tagline.** The setup screen spells out the acronym. That is fine for an 18+ app, but reviewers can flag profanity, so be ready to remove the tagline if asked.
6. **Real screenshots.** The captions above need images from a real device or simulator.
7. **Alcohol policies.** Both stores allow drinking games but ban content that encourages irresponsible drinking. The listing avoids "get drunk"-style wording on purpose. Keep any future updates in that tone.
